## Interpretations used (provisional; need reviewer confirmation)

- **I-1.** Ranges, one-sided bounds and direction-less specification values are documented information. They are never converted to an exact value and never rejected as absent; whether they serve a property's intended use is a provisional, property-specific decision (REPRESENTATIONS). Until decided, the requirement is not satisfied: the product is not Tier 1 complete; the information is reported in the documentation coverage.
- **I-2.** CCS "vs temperature" is satisfied by at least one CCS value with its test temperature.
- **I-3.** Component "primary and secondary function": the secondary function is optional; the primary is required.
- **I-4.** Function-specific markers are required for the functions the slide names (detergent: TBN + Ca or Mg; ZDDP: P + Zn; molybdenum: Mo). For other functions applicability is unresolved (not assumed inapplicable).
- **I-5.** VM and PPD subtype blocks are reported but not blocking, because they are stored in tier 2 / tier 3 files and the project rule says missing Tier 2/3 data does not block. This conflicts with slide 4 ("need response data on top") and is a decision for the reviewer (decision C).
- **I-6.** A treat rate or dose must be on a mass basis: stated in the recorded unit (wt.%, mass %) or established from the cited page (heading or footnote) in treat_rate_basis_evidence.json. A bare "%" without such evidence is missing its basis; the basis is never guessed.
- **I-7.** A response is usable only with its reference oil, dose and method (glossary extraction rule). A calculated value is accepted only if a registered check reproduces it from inputs printed on the cited page and its conditions match the response record.
- **I-8.** Package co-component architecture: met by a reported co-component with a mass-basis dose, or by a sourced statement that no co-component is required (proposed field co_component_requirement_statement; no record has one yet). Without either, applicability is unresolved; a co-component is never required merely because the schema cannot say "none".
