## New measures script (`scripts/08_progress_measures.py`)

It writes `tables/progress_measures.json` and `tables/document_read_scope.csv`, with the definitions in the script header.
- **Documents** are counted by unique SHA-256 and classified by **reading scope** only:
  - full read = a stated range pp.1-N covering every page, "(only page)", "all N pages", or a 1-page document read whole;
  - read in part = some other documented reading;
  - none = every link is still open.
- **Links** are counted by **coverage** (assessed / n/a / not assessed) and content review open/closed. Duplicate and
  non-duplicate links are kept apart.
- **Proposals** are counted as ready vs provisional; approved = 0.
- A document read in full does not make its links "coverage assessed". The table also counts full-read documents that still
  have unassessed links.
